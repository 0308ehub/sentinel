Customer Interview — Acme Corp
Date: May 15, 2026
Customer: Sarah Chen, Head of Operations
Plan: Enterprise ($50k/year)

INTERVIEWER: Tell me about your biggest frustrations with the current analytics tool.

SARAH: The number one issue is that I can't export my reports. Every time I need to share data with the exec team, I have to ask IT to run a custom export. It takes days. We're paying $50k/year and I can't download a CSV. This is blocking our workflows multiple times a week.

My team of 8 people all hit this issue. We've actually started using a competitor's tool just for the export functionality.

INTERVIEWER: What about permissions?

SARAH: That's the second issue. Our dashboards are accessible to our entire 200-person company, which is a compliance risk. Legal flagged it last quarter. We need role-based access control before we can expand the contract.

INTERVIEWER: Performance?

SARAH: 30-40 seconds to load a report. My team makes jokes about it. It's embarrassing to demo to my executives.

INTERVIEWER: Anything on your wish list?

SARAH: Scheduled report emails. Right now I manually export and email a report every Monday. If I could automate this, that would save me hours every week.
